## What this folder is

This `pitch/` folder is the strategic and product foundation for Discovr.

It is not just a pitch deck. It is the early company bible: the place where the core idea, product logic, feed philosophy, monetization, infrastructure assumptions, and long-term roadmap live before they become code.

Discovr is a normal social platform in the sense that users understand it immediately: scrolling feed, posts, profiles, likes, comments, follows, shares, messages, search, and communities.

The difference is the distribution model.

Discovr is built around one promise:

> Every post gets a real chance to be discovered.

The platform should feel familiar, but the algorithm should behave differently. Instead of only boosting people who are already popular, Discovr gives every post an initial test audience, measures meaningful signals, and expands reach based on quality, relevance, and connection potential.

## How to use this folder

Use this folder as the source of truth before building major features.

When Codex or a developer needs to understand what Discovr is, start here.

When a feature idea appears, add it to `09-open-decisions.md` first, then promote it into the correct chapter once the decision is made.

When the product changes, update these documents before or alongside the code.

## Current positioning

Discovr is not trying to reinvent the shape of social media.

It is trying to fix discovery.

Most platforms begin with popularity. Discovr begins with opportunity.
